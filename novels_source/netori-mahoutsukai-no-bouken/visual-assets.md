# Netori Mahoutsukai no Bouken — visual assets

Retrieved and checked: 2026-11-19. Author: **まじかり**; illustrator: **まくわうに**.

## Sources and identification

- Thai source: https://www.nekopost.net/novel/15549
- Original work: https://novel18.syosetu.com/n7524gn/
- Original title: `寝取り魔法使いの冒険 (Netori Mahoutsukai no Bouken)`
- Nekopost lists 14 Other Media covers (`V01`–`V14`). The volume 1 artwork is also
  chapter 0's `illust_02.jpg`, registered in `_source.json` as a source illustration.
- Character labels and names follow chapter 0 and the character-introduction chapters
  (0, 19.5 onward): อัลวิน, ชินจิ, เรนกะ, ชาร์ลอตต้า, อากาเนะ, มีริส, เฟรรี่.

The reader assets below are crops of official source illustrations. No AI-generated
artwork, recoloring, inpainting or speculative character matching is used. These files
are not added to `_source.json`; they are reader assets documented here only.
